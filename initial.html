Two constructions.

Paris, 1867 — one logic, called universal

Concentric rings of nations. Radial sectors of category. Every object assigned its exact place in a single hierarchy — and that hierarchy declared itself civilisation.

✦

This project reads those records through three lenses — the Official World, the Staged World, the Lived World — to show what the concentric logic concealed.

But this archive also makes choices. It too selects, names, and frames what matters. Every record here is someone's decision about what counts.
